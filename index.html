How It Works

From Fragmented Records to Audit-Ready Resolution in Hours

Every Case Flow deployment follows a proven four-stage dispute resolution journey from evidence ingestion and root-cause classification, to evidence graph construction, timeline reconstruction, and professional resolution pack generation all without requiring legal expertise from your finance or operations team.

STEP 01

Upload & Ingest

Import invoices, purchase orders, contracts, delivery notes and email exports. Structured capture with completeness checks ensures no critical document is missed.

STEP 02

Classify & Map

Our root-cause taxonomy automatically assigns dispute categories price mismatch, quality claim, missing PO and generates the required evidence checklist for each type.

STEP 03

Build Timeline

The Evidence Graph links every document to claims and events, building a single chronological "version of facts" that automatically flags missing artefacts and contradictions.

STEP 04

Resolve & Prevent

Generate a professional Resolution Package with quantified commercial settlement options. Closed disputes convert into operational controls that prevent future occurrences.

Core Capabilities

Five Engines. One Dispute Intelligence Layer.

Case Flow combines five proprietary workflow modules into a single dispute resolution operating system the only platform that unifies evidence ingestion, claim-level provenance modelling, automated timeline construction, sufficiency scoring, and prevention controls into one audit-ready resolution infrastructure for UK SMEs.

Engine 01

Evidence Ingestion Engine

Upload invoices, POs, contracts, delivery notes, email exports and photos. Structured capture with human-reviewed completeness checks ensures nothing critical is overlooked before resolution begins.

CSV invoice import for bulk dispute onboarding. Metadata capture with document-type classification. Provenance tagging links each artefact to the specific claim it supports or refutes creating machine-readable evidence relationships from day one.
Engine 02

Claim-Level Provenance Spine

Our proprietary data architecture organises every dispute as a logical relationship network Claims → Events → Artefacts. Unlike file attachment systems, every claim is traced to specific operational evidence with full provenance mapping.

Machine-readable evidence index with version history and optional file hashing for integrity verification. Contradictions are surfaced explicitly rather than buried in document bundles forcing structured resolution steps and eliminating interpretive ambiguity.
Engine 03

Evidence Sufficiency Scoring

Unlike AI tools that predict dispute outcomes, Case Flow measures evidence completeness. Each dispute type carries defined evidentiary requirements the system scores how well current evidence meets those standards, quantifying gaps before negotiation begins.

Rule-based sufficiency assessment for 12+ dispute root-cause categories including price mismatch, delivery variance, quality claims, missing POs, and acceptance disputes. Completeness scores allow finance teams to strengthen their position before any counterparty engagement.

Module 01

Evidence Ingestion & Normalisation

Upload invoices, POs, contracts, delivery notes, email exports and photos via drag-and-drop or CSV bulk import. Human-reviewed completeness checks flag missing artefacts before the workflow progresses.

Metadata capture with automated document-type classification. Structured import templates eliminate manual data entry errors. The ingestion layer converts raw commercial documents into structured, claim-linkable artefacts the foundational layer of the provenance architecture.
Module 02

Root-Cause Dispute Taxonomy Engine

Standard reason codes price mismatch, quantity variance, quality claim, missing PO, acceptance dispute each mapping automatically to required evidence checklists, workflow steps, and resolution output structure.

Classification acts as an operational compiler rather than a simple label. The taxonomy drives what evidence is collected, how the case progresses, and what format the resolution pack takes eliminating personal judgement and creating consistency across disputes, staff, and clients.
Module 03

Evidence Graph & Provenance Spine

Machine-readable model linking Documents → Events → Claims, creating provable provenance chains. Surfaces contradictions explicitly, forcing structured resolution steps and eliminating interpretive ambiguity between parties.

Version history with optional file hashing for document integrity verification. Immutable audit trail of all case actions and evidence submissions. The provenance spine is the core innovation a data architecture that has no direct equivalent in existing UK SME software categories.
Module 04

Chronological Timeline Builder

Automatically constructs a "single version of events" from contract execution through to dispute notice. Flags missing artefacts and contradictory data points in real-time as documents are linked to the timeline.

Chronological ordering is automatic, with manual override capability for complex disputed sequences. Each timeline entry is linked to its evidentiary source documents, making the final pack fully traceable and audit-ready for mediators, accountants, or commercial negotiators.
Module 05

Resolution Package Generator

Structured output pack: factual dispute summary, versioned evidence index, quantified commercial settlement options (partial payment, credit adjustment, staged settlement, re-performance), and template counterparty communications.

Legal-safe templates enforce factual documentation over interpretation allowing finance and operations staff to generate professional-grade resolution materials without inadvertently providing legal advice. Outputs are negotiation-ready and escalation-prepared commercial documents, not legal instruments.
Module 06

Dispute-to-Control Prevention Engine

Closed dispute data is analysed to extract root causes and evidence gaps, which are automatically converted into preventive operational controls e.g., require delivery proof before invoicing, mandate PO reference on all orders.

Over time, the prevention engine creates a feedback loop where resolved disputes actively reduce the number of future disputes. This is the only tool in the UK SME market that transforms dispute history into enforceable operational improvement intelligence making dispute management a continuous improvement process, not a reactive cost.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need legal expertise to use Case Flow?

+
No. Case Flow is specifically designed for finance managers, operations leads, and business owners without legal training. The workflow-guided interface with evidence checklists and legal-safe templates means you can build a professional-grade dispute pack without inadvertently providing legal advice. Our design principle: a finance team member should be able to manage a complex invoice dispute without external legal consultation at the pre-escalation stage.

How is Case Flow different from Xero, QuickBooks, or Chaser?

+
Accounting software like Xero and QuickBooks tracks transactions they don't manage disputes. Chaser automates payment reminders assuming invoices are valid it cannot reconstruct a contested transaction. Case Flow operates in an entirely different category: we model the evidence behind a dispute, build claim-provenance relationships, score evidence completeness, and generate commercial resolution packs. We are the operating system for the dispute workflow that sits between accounting software and legal escalation.

Will Case Flow provide legal advice or act as a legal service?

+
No. Case Flow is explicitly designed as an operational documentation tool, not a legal service. Our templates enforce factual documentation over interpretation, built-in disclaimers prevent advisory language, and the system is architected to create "legal-safe" outputs. The platform organises facts and evidence it does not predict legal outcomes, assess legal merit, or act as a substitute for solicitor advice when formal legal proceedings are required. This is a foundational design principle, not a caveat.

How does the Prevention Engine reduce future disputes?

+
Once a dispute is closed in Case Flow, the system analyses the root cause and evidence gaps identified during the case. These are converted into specific operational controls for example, "require signed delivery confirmation before generating invoice" or "mandate purchase order reference on all orders above £500." Over time, the dispute history creates a library of preventive controls that systematically reduce the internal process failures that generate disputes in the first place.
